Who it’s for

Made for the people who keep buildings running

Snagboard is for in-house facilities teams in organisations with several offices or sites, from one FM with two buildings to a national portfolio.

  • Facilities managersSee every site at a glance: what’s open, what’s overdue and what the landlord owes you.
  • Helpdesk teamsLog every request once, route it to the right person, and answer “is it fixed?” without a phone call.
  • Maintenance techniciansYour jobs on your phone. One big tap to move each one on, plus the right manual for the job.
  • Heads of FMPortfolio-wide oversight, controlled documents and ISO-ready evidence for audits.
Snagboard is a fit if…
  • You look after two or more buildings, offices or sites
  • Requests reach you by email, chat, phone and in person
  • You use a mix of in-house technicians and contractors
  • Some repairs are your landlord’s responsibility
  • You keep statutory, health & safety or ISO records

Typical teams: 2–30 sites, 1–20 technicians, a helpdesk of one or more.
